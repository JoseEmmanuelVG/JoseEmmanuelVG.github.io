**pAIy App** (later evolving into **Gubbi**) explores the intersection of **artificial intelligence**, **multi-chain finance**, and **community empowerment**.

<br />

The platform enables:
- **Transfers, payments, and CBDC swaps** across XRP Ledger, Solana, Ethereum (ETH-MX), and Base.
- **AI-generated financial content**: purchase descriptions, receipt images, and concise summaries.
- **Microloans**, **tokenization for small businesses**, **messaging**, **transaction history**, and **asset price tracking**.

<br />

<Callout type="info" title="Vision">
Not just payments — **Gubbi** turns financial access into practical tools communities can actually use.
</Callout>

<br />

---

<br />

## 🔑 Key Technologies

- **Blockchains:** XRP Ledger, Solana, Ethereum (ETH-MX), Base LATAM
- **AI / ML:** LLM-based text generation; AI images for receipts/UX hints
- **APIs & Cloud:** Microsoft Azure Cognitive Services, OpenAI API
- **Stack:** React, Next.js, Node.js, MongoDB

## 🌍 Impact

Across multiple hackathons and chains, **pAIy → Gubbi** validated a flexible, AI-assisted fintech approach that blends on-chain operations with human-friendly UX.  

It shows how **applied AI** can **explain**, **summarize**, and **augment** financial actions—lowering barriers to adoption while keeping technical rigor.

  <summary><strong>Notes & Learnings</strong> (click to expand)</summary>

- Chain-agnostic design helps move fast in hackathons while keeping a coherent UX.  
- AI content (descriptions, images) makes raw transactions more understandable for non-experts.  
- Careful key management and cost controls are essential when mixing LLMs and on-chain calls.
